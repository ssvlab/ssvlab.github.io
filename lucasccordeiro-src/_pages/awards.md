*These awards reflect more than ten years of work in software engineering, formal methods, and computer security. Since 2012, our tools -- especially the open-source bounded model checker ESBMC and related projects such as FuSeBMC -- have won 56 medals at the international competitions on software verification (SV-COMP) and software testing (Test-Comp), held with ETAPS.*
