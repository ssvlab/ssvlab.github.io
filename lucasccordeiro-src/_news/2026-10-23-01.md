Welcome to [Manan Mal](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/manan-mal-manchester) and [Mohd Ruhul Ameen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ameenruhul), who have joined our group at The University of Manchester as doctoral candidates in [GenAIDE](https://www.genaide.eu/), the Generative AI for Industrial Design Engineering Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Industrial Doctoral Network funded under Horizon Europe. Manan (DC7) works on robust code generation for simulation and optimization, and Ruhul (DC8) on LLM-based self-repair of simulation and optimization software; both projects extend [ESBMC](https://esbmc.org), so that one checks what the model generates and the other repairs it ([slides](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/talks/GenAIDE-2026-Manchester-Cordeiro.pdf)).
