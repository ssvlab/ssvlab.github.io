[FuSeBMC](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/papers/fase2021.pdf), our white-box fuzzer for finding security vulnerabilities in programs, won first place in the Cover-Error category and second place in the Overall category at [Test-Comp 2021](https://test-comp.sosy-lab.org/2021/). [JBMC](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/papers/cav2018.pdf), our verification tool for Java Bytecode, won third place in the Java overall category at [SV-COMP 2021](https://sv-comp.sosy-lab.org/2021/). [ESBMC](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/papers/ase2018.pdf), our SMT-based model checker for C/C++ programs, has successfully participated at [SV-COMP 2021](https://sv-comp.sosy-lab.org/2021/). It got first place in one sub-category, second place in four sub-categories, fourth place in the ReachSafety category, fifth place in the SoftwareSystems category. Overall, the sixth place among 25 state-of-the-art software verifiers at [SV-COMP 2021](https://sv-comp.sosy-lab.org/2021/)!
