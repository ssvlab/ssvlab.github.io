[FuSeBMC](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/papers/fase2021.pdf), our white-box fuzzer for finding security vulnerabilities in programs, won three gold medals in the 8th Intl. Competition on Software Testing ([Test-Comp 2026](https://test-comp.sosy-lab.org/2026/)) at FASE'26. [ESBMC](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/papers/ase2018.pdf), our SMT-based model checker for C/C++ programs, won one gold and one silver medal in the 15th Intl. Competition on Software Verification ([SV-COMP 2026](https://sv-comp.sosy-lab.org/2026/)) at TACAS'26, ETAPS 2026, Turin, Italy.
