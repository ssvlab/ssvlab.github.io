Tong Wu passed his Ph.D. viva with minor corrections, defending the thesis **Efficient Concurrent Software Verification with Bounded Model Checking and Deep Learning**. The external examiner was Prof. [Ajitha Rajan](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/persons/ajitha-rajan/) (University of Edinburgh) and the internal examiner Dr. [Konstantin Korovin](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/persons/konstantin-korovin/) (University of Manchester); the thesis was supervised with [Pavlos Petoumenos](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/persons/pavlos.petoumenos). Dr. Wu is the 14th Ph.D. student from our group to graduate in formal software verification, and works on our software verification spinout [VeriBee](https://www.veribee.co/).
