Visited Minas Gerais, Brazil, between 15 and 24 June 2026. Gave the invited talk "From Research to Impact: Software Verification, Veribee, and Manchester–Brazil Opportunities" and a [two-session formal verification workshop](https://github.com/lucasccordeiro/unifei), built on our [Software Security](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/courses/2022/01/software-security/index.html) course, at the Universidade Federal de Itajubá (UNIFEI); presented collaboration perspectives on formal methods, software verification, and systems security for critical sectors at the [Instituto Nacional de Telecomunicações (Inatel)](https://inatel.br/) and the Inatel Competence Center in Santa Rita do Sapucaí; and lectured on academic, scientific, and technological partnership opportunities with the UK at UNIFEI Pouso Alegre and Senac Pouso Alegre, with a class for SENAC Tech and UNIFEI Cybersecurity students.
