[FuSeBMC](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/papers/fase2021.pdf), our white-box fuzzer for finding security vulnerabilities in programs, won first place in the Cover-Error, Cover-Branches and Overall categories at [Test-Comp 2022](https://test-comp.sosy-lab.org/2022/). [JBMC](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/papers/cav2018.pdf), our verification tool for Java Bytecode, won second place in the Java overall category at [SV-COMP 2022](https://sv-comp.sosy-lab.org/2022/). [ESBMC](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/papers/ase2018.pdf), our SMT-based model checker for C/C++ programs, has successfully participated at [SV-COMP 2022](https://sv-comp.sosy-lab.org/2022/). It got second place in three sub-categories, third in one sub-category, fourth in the ReachSafety category, seventh place among 40 state-of-the-art software verifiers at [SV-COMP 2022](https://sv-comp.sosy-lab.org/2022/)!
