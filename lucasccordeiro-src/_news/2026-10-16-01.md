Gave the invited talk ["Formal Verification of LLM-Generated Code"](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/talks/fattw2026_slides.pdf) at [FATTW 2026](https://conf.researchr.org/home/fattw-2026), the invitation-only workshop on the new foundations of software testing co-located with ASE 2026 in Munich, Germany, on how formal verification can check the code that large language models generate.
