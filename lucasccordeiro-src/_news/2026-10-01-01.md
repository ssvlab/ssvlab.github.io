An uncontrolled-recursion vulnerability we found in the Ion reader of [Amazon Ion Python](https://github.com/amazon-ion/ion-python) using [ESBMC](https://esbmc.org) has been assigned [CVE-2026-104020](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-104020) ([GHSA-93q6-f8hx-vv7f](https://github.com/amazon-ion/ion-python/security/advisories/GHSA-93q6-f8hx-vv7f)), and is fixed in v0.15.0. Joint work with Weiqi Wang.
