Gave the invited talk ["Verifying What Generative AI Builds: Formal Methods for the AI Partner in Engineering"](https://ssvlab.github.io/lucasccordeiro/talks/GenAIDE-2026-Manchester-Cordeiro.pdf) for [GenAIDE](https://www.genaide.eu/), the Generative AI for Industrial Design Engineering Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Industrial Doctoral Network funded under Horizon Europe, at The University of Manchester, setting out how formal methods can check what generative models produce when AI becomes a partner in the design and engineering loop.
