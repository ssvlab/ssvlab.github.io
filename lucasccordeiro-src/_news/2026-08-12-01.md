Two memory-safety vulnerabilities we found in the Base64 decoder of the [AWS SDK for C++](https://github.com/aws/aws-sdk-cpp) using [ESBMC](https://esbmc.org) have been assigned [CVE-2026-19642](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-19642) and [CVE-2026-19643](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-19643), and are fixed in v1.11.862. Joint work with Rafael Sá Menezes.
